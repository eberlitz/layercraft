# Attribution

LayerCraft is an original, clean-room 3D printer slicer written in pure Rust and dual-licensed under `MIT OR Apache-2.0`.

## Inspirations & Architectural Patterns
- Governance, layered workspace checks (`cargo xtask layers`), license auditing (`cargo xtask licences`), and black-box oracle testing patterns were inspired by [SolidCraft](https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcraft) (`MIT OR Apache-2.0`).

## Third-Party Crates
All shipped Rust dependencies and their permissive licenses are recorded in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt` (generated and verified by `cargo xtask licences`).
